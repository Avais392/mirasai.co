Cookie Policy

A plain-language record of the browser storage used on mirasai.co. The current website does not use advertising cookies or analytics cookies.

Effective

Last updated

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Miras AI website at mirasai.co. It should be read with our Privacy Policy, which explains how we handle personal information received through enquiries, recruitment and normal website operations.

2. What the website stores

The website uses browser local storage only to remember whether you accepted or declined the cookie notice. Local storage is data saved by your browser on your device. It is not sent to Miras AI with each web request.

This preference is not used to identify you, follow you across websites or build an advertising profile.

3. Cookies currently used

Miras AI does not currently set analytics, advertising or behavioral-tracking cookies on this website. The notice records only your local preference. If we introduce optional analytics or marketing technologies, we will update this policy and obtain consent where applicable before activating them.

4. External links

The site links to LinkedIn but does not embed LinkedIn tracking technology. LinkedIn receives information only if you choose to open its website, at which point its own terms and privacy notice apply.

5. Your controls

You can remove the saved preference through your browser’s site-data or local-storage controls. Clearing it causes the cookie notice to appear again. Blocking local storage will not prevent access to the site, although the notice choice may not persist between visits.

6. Changes to this policy

We will revise this page when the website’s storage or tracking practices change. The effective and last-updated dates at the top show when the current version took effect.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy or the technologies used on the site may be sent to avais@mirasai.co.
